# AI product video ads for online stores with Studio Ads

> Kineo Studio Ads turns a product photo or link into an ad of about 15 seconds with voice, music and on-screen text.
> Choose from 17 product styles, optionally add a clearly identified AI person, and queue batches of up to 10 ads.
> Review the product image, claims and chosen format before publishing; generated scenes are not customer testimonials.

## From a product listing to a useful ad

Studio Ads uses your product photo, accurate description and destination link to create a short advertisement. Start with a photo or product link, inspect the information collected and review the proposed scenes before rendering.

A link is a starting point, not a substitute for checking the product. Confirm the item, images, price and offer. If the importer cannot obtain the correct information, supply your own authorized photo and wording. Never assume a generated script knows current inventory or shipping terms.

The [Business page](https://www.usekineo.com/business) describes this product-photo workflow; [Studio Ads](https://www.usekineo.com/ads) explains planning, levels and delivery. Both were checked on October 14, 2026. The result is creative material, not campaign management.

## Choose a style that helps identify the product

The current catalogue contains **17 product effects**, with an optional AI person as a separate choice. Effects include package explosion, product close-up, studio lighting, supermarket shelf, illustrated poster, parachute delivery and candy machine. You can also choose Auto without an effect. The style applies to a product shot rather than turning every scene into the same effect.

For an illustrative perfume brief, product close-up or studio lighting could emphasize the bottle's appearance. For a packaged snack, a supermarket setting could communicate its retail context. These are suggested creative directions, not customer case studies or evidence that a style improves conversion.

Inspect the package after generation. Small lettering, proportions and surfaces need checking. An attractive effect is not useful if shoppers cannot recognize the item they will receive.

## An AI person must remain identifiable as AI

Studio Ads can add a synthetic person holding the product and speaking the script. Treat that person as an AI presenter, never as a real customer or someone who actually used the product.

Keep the wording factual. Explain a supplied feature or invite viewers to inspect the listing. Do not invent personal experience, a review or a result. Kineo displays an AI-label reminder when the ad is delivered; the reminder is not an automatically burned-in disclosure. Clearly identify the synthetic presenter and enable the applicable platform disclosure when posting.

The presenter option requires a product photo and narration. It is currently vertical-only. Review the spoken delivery and how the product appears in the person's hands.

## Formats, batches and the real product at the end

The available frame choices are **9:16, 4:5 and 1:1**, with restrictions. Product effects and the AI presenter require 9:16. Cinema does not support 4:5. Choose a compatible combination before rendering; selecting a format does not deliver all three versions together.

Batch mode queues up to **10 ads**, subject to access and sufficient balance. Each item is charged when it starts. This is a queue, not a promise of ten simultaneous renders. Review the individual briefs before committing the batch.

With a usable product photo, the workflow includes an untouched product-photo shot and can place the real photo on the closing card. Check that closing image before posting. Keeping a real image visible helps customers identify the actual product after the illustrative scenes.

## When NOT to use this

Do not use generated imagery as proof of product performance, a real customer endorsement or a precise technical demonstration. Record real footage when the claim depends on showing what the item actually does.

Avoid batching unreviewed product information or choosing an effect that obscures the package. If you require every scene to remain visually identical to an original photo, a generative effect is the wrong choice. Studio Ads creates advertising material; it does not guarantee ad approval, sales or return on advertising spend.

## FAQs

### Can I start with only one product photo?

Yes. The current product workflow accepts a product photo as a starting point, and you can also begin with a product link. Use a clear, authorized image of the actual item. Review the imported information and proposed scenes before rendering, especially if the listing contains several variants or products.

### Are the 17 styles all available in every format?

No. The product effects currently require vertical 9:16. The workflow also offers 4:5 and square 1:1 for compatible configurations, but the AI presenter is vertical-only and Cinema does not support 4:5. Choose the format and creative options together rather than assuming one selection can produce every combination.

### Is the AI person a real customer testimonial?

No. The person is synthetic and must not be presented as a real customer, reviewer or product user. Use approved product facts rather than invented personal experience. Kineo shows a disclosure reminder at delivery; clearly identify the AI presenter and apply the relevant platform labeling when you publish the ad.

### How many product ads can Business cover?

The Business plan supplies 500 credits for $84 per month. At the Commercial level's 41 credits per approximately 15-second ad, that covers twelve ads if the balance is used only for those productions. Different levels, retakes and other creations change the count; twelve ads and 500 credits are not separate allowances.

### Can I generate ten ads at once?

You can prepare a batch of up to ten ads, within your access and available balance. The system queues them and charges each item when it starts, rather than promising ten parallel renders. Review each product, script and destination first so that a mistaken detail is not repeated across a batch.

### Will the final ad show my actual product photo?

With a usable product photo, the workflow includes an unaltered photo shot and supports the real image on the closing card. Review the ending to confirm that the correct item is visible. This does not guarantee perfect product fidelity in the generated scenes that appear earlier in the advertisement.

## Prepare one product before scaling

Open [Studio Ads](https://www.usekineo.com/ads) with one accurate product listing, choose a compatible style and format, then inspect the finished ad. Expand to a batch only after the product, message and closing image meet your review criteria.
