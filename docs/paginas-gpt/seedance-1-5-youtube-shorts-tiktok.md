## What you are buying

If you searched for somewhere to use Seedance 1.5 for YouTube Shorts or TikTok, start by deciding whether you need a single scene or a finished video. Kineo offers both, but this guide concerns the finished film: script, generated visuals, narration, captions and music assembled into an MP4. You can start with an idea or bring a script.

Seedance 1.5 Pro is a ByteDance video model; see the [official model page](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/seedance1_5_pro), accessed October 14, 2026. It generates the scenes inside Kineo's workflow. Kineo handles the surrounding production. Its Seedance Shorts workflow uses separate narration rather than relying on the model's own audio. That distinction matters when you want a coherent explanation across several shots instead of one isolated visual experiment. See the [Seedance engine page](https://www.usekineo.com/ai-video-generator/seedance), accessed October 14, 2026.

For a vertical channel, choose 9:16 before rendering. Decide the subject, spoken language and intended length before spending credits. A selected duration is a production target, so review the actual exported runtime if your placement requires a precise length.

## Three useful ways to use it

For a mystery channel, start with a specific question and build an explanation around it. The public Seedance gallery includes a Mary Celeste film. Use that as a production reference, not as a verified historical source: research the story independently before approving narration.

For a geography explainer, an illustrative brief might ask for a vertical overview of an unusual landscape, followed by closer visual details. The published Darvaza example shows the kind of topic Kineo presents. Keep the distinction between generated scenery and documentary footage visible wherever it matters.

For a recurring educational series, preserve the channel's narration style and structure while changing the subject. An illustrative episode could explain one everyday phenomenon using a clear opening, visual progression and conclusion. This is a suggested workflow, not a claim that a particular prompt has delivered audience growth.

The practical reason to start with Seedance 1.5 is its lower published credit cost among the engines compared here. Spend on a more expensive model when you can identify the visual requirement that justifies it.

## Write for a complete Short

A useful brief names the subject, the question being answered and the visual setting. “Explain why this abandoned harbor was important, with generated historical illustrations” gives the production a clearer job than “make something cinematic.”

Review names, dates and claims before rendering. Afterwards, check captions, pronunciation, scene relevance and whether the ending lands naturally. Kineo automates assembly, but publishing still requires an editorial decision. Do not treat generated architecture, uniforms or machinery as a faithful reconstruction by default.

## When NOT to use this

Choose a different workflow when your main material is a podcast or interview you already filmed and you need excerpts. This from-scratch film workflow is not a long-recording clipper.

Do not choose Seedance 1.5 specifically for a character delivering dialogue on camera with lip sync. Kineo's Kling 3 workflow addresses that different requirement, with its own limits. Also reconsider if you need exact frame-by-frame editing, guaranteed continuity or an unaltered record of a real event. Those needs exceed what this page promises.

## FAQs

### Is Seedance 1.5 the same as Seedance 2.5?

No. Kineo lists them as separate engines with separate credit costs. A 60-second Seedance 1.5 film uses 35 credits, while Seedance 2.5 uses 150. Choose from the actual engine selector and review examples; a newer model name alone does not establish that it is the right purchase for your video.

### Can I use Seedance 1.5 for both Shorts and TikTok?

Yes. Choose the vertical 9:16 format and download the finished film for your intended platform. Check the actual runtime, captions and framing before uploading. Creating a suitable file does not guarantee distribution, audience response or eligibility for a platform's monetization program, and this guide makes no such promise.

### How much money does a 60-second Seedance film cost?

The film consumes 35 credits. Using the regular Creator plan's $19.90 price and 150 monthly credits gives a $4.64 allocation. That is a way to compare usage, not a $4.64 checkout offer. Your cash payment depends on the plan or eligible credit purchase you select.

### Can I supply my own script?

Yes. Kineo supports starting from an existing script as well as an idea. Review the chosen workflow, language and duration before generating, especially if the wording must remain precise. Listen to the exported narration and check captions before publishing; an input script is not proof that every output detail is correct.

### Does every Seedance film include a watermark?

Paid plans unlock clean, watermark-free MP4 exports. The new-account film trial is watermarked and subject to the eligibility described on pricing. If your purpose is regular paid production, evaluate the plan balance against the engine and duration you will actually use rather than treating trial access as an ongoing allowance.

### Is Seedance 1.5 suitable for factual documentaries?

It can illustrate a narrated explanation, but generated visuals are not documentary evidence. Verify the script using reliable sources and disclose synthetic reconstruction where appropriate. If the audience needs the real location, event or product exactly as recorded, use authorized real footage and an editing workflow that preserves it.
