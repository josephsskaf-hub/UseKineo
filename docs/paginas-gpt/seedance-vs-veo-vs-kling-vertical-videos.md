# Seedance vs Veo vs Kling for Vertical Videos

> Seedance 1.5 is the lowest-credit option here for a finished narrated vertical video on Kineo.
> Veo 3.1 Fast suits a visual brief to review closely; Kling 2.5 suits motion-led stories; Kling 3 supports English on-camera dialogue.
> A 60-second film costs 35, 100, 60 or 150 credits respectively; choose by the story, not the model name.

## Compare the finished video you need

A model comparison becomes useful when it describes the deliverable. On Kineo, a clip is a single generated scene; a film combines scenes, narration, captions and music into a vertical MP4. This page compares finished films for YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Reels. Buying raw model output elsewhere means making separate decisions about editing, voice and captions. [Kineo pricing](https://www.usekineo.com/pricing), accessed October 14, 2026.

Start with the story. A narrated historical mystery needs illustrative scenes. An action sequence puts movement at the center. A conversation needs visible speakers whose lines make sense on screen. Spending more on the engine does not resolve an unclear script, establish historical accuracy or guarantee audience retention.

## Supplier features are not the same as Kineo features

ByteDance describes Seedance 1.5 Pro as a joint audio-video model with lip synchronization and cinematic camera movement. Those are supplier capabilities and claims, not proof that every control or audio mode appears in Kineo. [ByteDance model page](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/seedance1_5_pro), accessed October 14, 2026.

Google documents native audio, portrait output and short native clip durations for Veo 3.1. Kineo's narrated-film integration uses Veo 3.1 Fast, switches off that model audio and adds its own narration, captions and music. A request for Veo's native conversation is therefore different from ordering this finished Short. [Google documentation](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/veo?hl=en) and [Kineo's Veo page](https://www.usekineo.com/ai-video-generator/veo), accessed October 14, 2026.

Kling's supplier documents multi-shot generation and native dialogue for version 3.0. This does not establish those capabilities for version 2.5. Nor does supplier multilingual support establish multilingual dialogue inside Kineo. [Kling 3.0 guide](https://kling.ai/quickstart/klingai-video-3-model-user-guide), accessed October 14, 2026.

## When NOT to use this

Do not use an engine leaderboard to choose a tool for exact product demonstrations, factual evidence or identity-critical footage. Review generated details against reliable references. If a shot must prove what a real object did, film the object.

Avoid paying for Kling 3 merely because its version number is higher. Avoid choosing Veo solely for native audio when ordering Kineo's narrated workflow. If you need manual control over every shot or supplier-specific controls, verify those controls before spending credits.

## FAQs

### Which engine should I choose first?

Start with the outcome. For a narrated vertical story without visible dialogue, Seedance 1.5 offers the lowest credit cost among these four Kineo options. Choose a more expensive engine when its workflow addresses a specific scene requirement. Review the first finished result before committing the rest of your allowance.

### Does a more expensive model produce a better Short?

Not automatically. A higher credit price describes what you spend, not what viewers will watch. Compare intelligibility, pacing, visual consistency and how well the scenes support your script. An attractive shot can still distract from the story. Treat this comparison as a selection guide, not a measured ranking of audience response.

### Can all four engines make characters talk?

Do not assume so from supplier marketing. Kineo presents Kling 3 as its talking-character option within this comparison, while its Kling 2.5 and Veo film workflows use separate narration. Check the current engine page and dialogue language before ordering. Supplier demonstrations do not establish identical behavior in a finished-film integration.

### Are these dollar amounts the price I pay for one video?

They are credit-value equivalents, calculated from the regular Creator monthly plan, rather than standalone purchases. Your checkout pays for the selected plan or credit package. The effective allocation changes with that purchase. Check the current pricing page, available balance and planned duration before deciding how many films your budget covers.

### Is Seedance 2.5 the same option as Seedance 1.5?

No. Kineo lists them separately, with different credit requirements and positioning. This comparison deliberately uses Seedance 1.5 as the lower-cost narrated option. Do not transfer its prices or measured turnaround to Seedance 2.5. Read the separate engine page when considering a production that depends on the newer model's particular strengths.

### How should I compare results fairly?

Keep the script, intended duration and creative brief consistent, then inspect whether each result communicates the same idea clearly. Note visible errors, understandable speech and usable scenes. Record credits spent and completion time, including rejected attempts. A personal trial can guide your next purchase, but it cannot establish a universal model ranking.

Review the [current plans](https://www.usekineo.com/pricing), then choose an engine in [Kineo Studio](https://www.usekineo.com/studio) for a script with a clear visual purpose.
