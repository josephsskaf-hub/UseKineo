# How Much Does an AI Short Video Cost in 2026?

> Kineo prices narrated AI Shorts by engine and duration, using one shared credit balance.
> A 15-second Seedance 1.5 film costs 9 credits; a 60-second Kling 3 film costs 150 credits.
> At the regular Creator rate, those credits allocate approximately $1.19 and $19.90, respectively.

## First, define what you are buying

“An AI video” can mean a single generated scene or a finished narrated Short. Separating those deliverables prevents misleading comparisons.

Kineo's pricing page distinguishes clips from narrated films. A clip is one scene from text or a photo. A narrated film combines script, voice, scenes and captions; the page also lists music among the included film features. Paid plans provide clean, watermark-free 9:16 MP4 downloads.

The table below concerns narrated films. It does not price a standalone provider clip, paid advertising distribution or a human production service. Your preferred engine and target duration determine the credit charge. [Kineo pricing](https://www.usekineo.com/pricing), checked October 14, 2026.

## Match the budget to a usable balance

On the same date, regular monthly plans are Starter at $9.90 for 60 credits, Creator at $19.90 for 150, and Studio at $54.90 for 300. Annual billing and the advertised first-month promotion are separate offers; neither is the basis of this table. [Current plans](https://www.usekineo.com/pricing), checked October 14, 2026.

A Creator balance can fund four 60-second Seedance films, leaving 10 credits, or one 60-second Kling 3 film. Those are alternative uses of the same balance. Spending on clips, images or other work reduces what remains.

If you buy a subscription and use only one film, your actual spending still includes the unused credits.

## How Runway and Adobe Firefly compare

These alternatives suit generated-footage workflows. The figures describe specific plans and generation settings, not equivalent finished Shorts.

| Service and selected offer | Verified cost basis | What the comparison covers |
|---|---|---|
| Runway Standard | $12/month equivalent, billed $144 annually; 625 monthly credits | Gen-4.5 uses 60 credits for five seconds: ≈$1.15 allocated from this plan |
| Adobe Firefly Standard | $9.99/month; 2,000 monthly credits | Firefly Video at 1080p and 24 fps uses 100 credits/second: a five-second clip consumes 500 credits, ≈$2.50 allocated |

Sources: [Runway pricing](https://runway.com/pricing), [Adobe Firefly plans](https://www.adobe.com/products/firefly/plans.html?locale=en_US), and [Adobe's generative-credit documentation](https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-cloud/apps/generative-ai/generative-credits-faq.html), all accessed October 14, 2026.

Runway's example uses annual billing; Adobe's uses monthly billing. Adobe's credits change with resolution. Neither calculated clip cost is a standalone purchase.

Do not multiply five-second clip prices and call the result a guaranteed finished-film quote. Editing, narration, usable takes and remaining credits affect the project. Compare the deliverable you actually need: individual footage for an editing workflow, or Kineo's assembled narrated film.

## When NOT to use this

Do not use these allocations as a fixed production quotation when the exact scene is essential and you have no room for revision. A generation budget cannot guarantee that the first result will meet your brief.

Do not pick the highest-priced engine solely because it costs more. Price is verified here; a controlled quality ranking across these engines is not. If you already have all your footage and only need editing, buying new AI-generated scenes may add unnecessary cost.

For product-photo advertisements, use the separate Studio Ads offer and its displayed credits. The narrated-film table above does not price that workflow.

## FAQs

### Which premium Kineo engine costs the least?

Among the six engines listed in Kineo's public pricing table on October 14, 2026, Seedance 1.5 has the lowest narrated-film credit cost at 15 and 60 seconds. That is a price comparison, not proof that it delivers the best result for every subject or visual style.

### How much does a 15-second AI Short cost?

For the six Kineo engines shown here, a 15-second narrated film costs between 9 and 38 credits. At the regular Creator allocation, that is approximately $1.19 to $5.04. Your cash payment is for the plan or credits you purchase, while the selected generation consumes credits from your available balance.

### Can I buy one video for the dollar amount in the table?

These amounts are calculated allocations, not individual video checkout offers. They divide the regular Creator subscription price by its monthly credits and multiply by the generation's credit cost. Check Kineo's current plans or one-time credit options for the amount you must actually pay to obtain a sufficient balance.

### Why are the 35-second prices marked separately?

Kineo's public engine-price table displays 15, 30, 60 and 90 seconds, with unavailable combinations marked. The 35-second column here comes from the current duration-based billing rule, rounded up to a whole credit. It is clearly identified so readers do not mistake a calculated figure for a displayed pricing-page cell.

### Does a more expensive engine guarantee a better Short?

No. The table establishes what each engine costs, not an outcome guarantee. A suitable script, visual brief and acceptable generated result still matter. Choose according to the kind of film you want, then review the output. There is no verified universal quality ranking in this comparison, or promise of views, sales or earnings.

### Are Runway and Firefly prices directly equivalent to Kineo's?

No. The examples above allocate subscription credits to generated clips under specified settings. Kineo's film table describes an assembled narrated output. A fair project comparison includes whatever editing, voice, captions and additional generations your workflow requires. Provider credit systems and billing commitments also differ, so their raw credit counts are not interchangeable.
