### Descrição longa (200–300 palavras)
```
Kineo turns one typed topic — or a script you already wrote — into a finished
faceless 9:16 Short. It writes the script, generates the AI voiceover, matches
visuals to every narration line, burns in captions and renders a finished MP4.
There is no footage to upload and no editing timeline: it creates from scratch
rather than clipping a long video you already recorded.

Eight engines share one credit balance, from the free Kineo 1 through Seedance
1.5, Kling 2.5, Veo 3.1, MiniMax H3, Omni Flash, Kling 3 and an AI Avatar with

piece cost what they actually cost instead of everything costing the same.

the next five episodes of that story as a season, so a first video becomes a
series instead of a one-off — writing the season costs nothing and spends no
credits, and an episode is only charged if you actually render it. And every
finished film ships with the copy needed to publish it: YouTube title and
description, TikTok caption with hashtags, and a pinned comment, ready to paste.

New accounts get 25 free credits with every engine unlocked and no credit card.
Trial films carry a watermark; any paid plan exports clean MP4s. Paid plans
start at $7 a month. You keep ownership of every video you generate.
```

## O QUE MEDIR DEPOIS, e como não se enganar

Cada diretório manda tráfego com referrer próprio. Depois de submeter, o placar
por fonte sai de `docs/queries/MAPA-DE-ENTRADA-2026-09-06.sql`.

**A armadilha:** o número que vai crescer primeiro é **cadastros**, e ele vai
parecer ótimo. O TAAFT já provou que cadastro de diretório não vira assinatura.
Olhe **`checkout` e `pagou`** na mesma linha da tabela — e só considere um
diretório "funcionando" quando ele produzir **checkout**, não quando produzir
cadastro.
