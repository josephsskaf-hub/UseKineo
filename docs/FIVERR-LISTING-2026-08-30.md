## FAQ

### Are these videos made with AI?

Yes. The workflow uses generative AI and/or licensed stock media for scripts, voice and visuals. The final files are reviewed as a batch before delivery.

### Can I publish them commercially?

Yes. The delivered MP4 files can be published by your business or delivered to your client. The service does not transfer access to the Kineo software itself.

### Do you edit my existing footage?

No. This offer creates faceless vertical videos from topics, approved scripts or source material. It is not a source-footage editing service.

### What does one revision include?

One adjustment to the brief or scripts before generation. A new topic or a request to rebuild already generated scenes is a new scope.

### Can you use a real celebrity or public figure?

No impersonation or deceptive depiction. A factual documentary topic may use properly licensed or generated contextual visuals without pretending the person endorsed the video.
