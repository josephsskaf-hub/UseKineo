FAQ

Common questions

Is the video really mine to post?

Yes. Never-paid free users can download, share and post the watermarked MP4. Paid plans unlock the clean, watermark-free MP4 for YouTube, TikTok or Reels.

Do I need any editing skills?

None. You type one idea and the AI writes the script, records the voice, generates every scene and adds captions. Free downloads carry a watermark; paid plans unlock the clean MP4.

Is there a watermark?

New accounts get 10 credits — enough for your first 15-second AI film; trial films are watermarked. Paid plans export clean, watermark-free MP4s.

Can Kineo also generate images and voiceovers?

Yes — Kineo includes an AI image studio (6 engines including FLUX, Seedream and Nano Banana Pro, from 1 credit per image) and a voice studio with 4 text-to-speech engines (from 1 credit per 1000 characters). Everything you make lives in your Library.

Can I make my videos sharper?

Every video has a one-click Enhance option powered by Topaz film restoration — it removes compression artifacts, recovers detail and adds fine cinematic grain. 10 credits per video; the Studio plan includes 2 free enhances a month.

Can I use my own script?

Yes — paste your script and pick “Use my script as is” and the AI narrates it word for word.

What if a generation fails?

Your credits come back automatically the moment a render fails — no support ticket, no waiting. You only pay for videos you actually get.

Can I cancel anytime?

Anytime, in one click. Plans are month to month and your credits refresh every month.

Can I run a whole channel with the same host?

Yes — that’s the point. Keep the same voice, style and captions across every episode so your channel looks consistent, without filming a single frame.

Can I monetize the videos?

Yes. Every video is yours to keep, post and monetize — including the YouTube Partner Program, TikTok and Reels. No extra license needed.

Can I use the videos commercially, or for client work?

Yes. Our terms let you use Kineo for lawful personal or commercial purposes and confirm that you keep ownership of the videos you generate, so you can post them, monetize them and deliver them to a client as part of your own paid service. No extra license, no per-video royalty. Two limits come from the same terms: you cannot resell or redistribute Kineo itself, and the stock clips inside a render are licensed for use in your finished video, not for re-upload as standalone stock footage. Paid plans export the clean, watermark-free MP4.

How long does one video take?

A Seedance 1.5 film takes a measured median of 5.9 minutes: every scene is generated by AI, then narrated, scored and composed into the final MP4. Premium engines can take longer.

How much does Kineo cost?

Kineo has three monthly plans: Starter at $9.00 for 40 credits, Creator at $19.00 for 150 credits and Studio at $59.00 for 470 credits. Credits are spent per video and how many a video costs depends on the engine you pick, so a Fast render and a cinematic film come out of the same balance at very different rates. Kineo lists reference plan prices in USD. Customers in Brazil pay in reais (BRL) at checkout. Elsewhere, the checkout shows the final charge currency; your bank may add conversion or cross-border fees. Every new account in supported countries starts free with 10 credits — enough for your first 15-second AI film, no card.

Which AI video engines can I use in Kineo?

Five, behind one interface and one balance: Kling 4.0, MiniMax H3, MiniMax H3 Max, MiniMax H3 Turbo and Seedance 2.5 (paid plans). You choose the engine per video, so a cheap explainer and a cinematic flagship can come out of the same account on the same day. Every clip on this page is a real render from the engine named on the card — the badge always tells the truth about which model made it.

What is the best AI video generator for faceless YouTube channels?

It depends on whether you want stock footage assembled or footage generated. Tools like InVideo and AutoShorts cut stock clips to your script, which is cheaper and fine for talking-point videos. Kineo generates the footage with models such as Seedance 2.5 and Kling 4.0, keeps your narration word for word instead of rewriting it, and targets 60 seconds or more so the video qualifies for TikTok Creator Rewards. If your channel lives on visuals nobody else has, generation wins; if it lives on volume, stock is cheaper.
