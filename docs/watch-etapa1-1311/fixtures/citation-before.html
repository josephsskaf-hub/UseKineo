What do I need to start a film?

An idea or your own script.

What is included?

The script, AI narration, generated scenes, captions and music.

Is the free film watermark-free?

Paid plans provide clean MP4 downloads. The trial film has a watermark.

What does the entry plan cost?

Starter is US$9/month as the USD reference price, with 40 monthly credits. Customers in Brazil pay in reais at checkout.

What else can I make?

3D kids cartoons, product ads and ads with an AI presenter have separate workflows.
