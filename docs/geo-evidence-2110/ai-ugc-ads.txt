 In short
 A UGC ad ("user-generated content") is the format that looks filmed on a phone by an ordinary person. On Kineo the person on screen is an AI presenter, never a real customer, and the ad is delivered with a reminder to label it as AI.
 You send 1 to 7 real photos of the product and one sentence (or the product link). The presenter holds your product and says what it is, who it is for and why to buy it now, while your photos cut in between the lines.
 The first UGC ad is free (one per account, 15 seconds, watermarked). After that, a 15-second ad uses 41 credits from any paid plan.
 How it works, in 3 steps
 Show your product
 One sentence about the product (or your store link), your logo and 1 to 7 real photos. The script uses only the facts you give it.
 Pick the presenter
 Choose one of the approved presenters or let Kineo choose. You see the plan and the script before anything is charged: planning is free.
 Get your ad
 The presenter speaks with a lip-synced voice, your photos cut in between the lines, music plays underneath and your logo closes the ad. Download the vertical MP4 and post it anywhere.

 What the presenter says (and never says)
 Talks TO the viewer: what the product is, the main benefit, who it is for and why to get it now.
 Never poses as a customer: no "I bought", "I use it", "my skin", and no before-and-after.
 Never asks viewers to smell, feel or close their eyes: on camera that reads as fake.
 Uses only the facts you gave: no invented ratings, customer counts or awards.
 TikTok, Meta and YouTube ask for realistic AI people to be labeled: turn on the platform’s AI label when you post.

 Frequently asked questions
 What is a UGC ad? UGC means "user-generated content": a phone-style video with a person talking straight to the camera. As an ad it usually feels less like advertising than a studio commercial. On Kineo the person is an AI presenter.
 Is the person in the ad a real customer? No. It is an AI presenter, and the script never speaks as a customer. That is why the ad carries no testimonial, rating or personal result, and why you should turn on the platform’s AI label when you post.
 How much does an AI UGC ad cost? The first one is free (one per account, 15 seconds, watermarked). On paid plans a 15-second ad uses 41 credits, 20 seconds 55 and 30 seconds 82. Plans start at $9.90 per month.
 Can I use the ad commercially? Who owns the video? Yes. Kineo’s terms allow personal or commercial use and say you retain ownership of the videos you generate. The free sample carries a watermark; paid plans download without it. Your product photos and claims must be yours or authorized.
 Which languages can the presenter speak? The script and the voice follow the language you write in, across Kineo’s 17 narration languages. We have reviewed presenter ads in English and Portuguese; in other languages, listen before you post.
 What format and length do I get? Vertical 9:16 for Reels, TikTok, Stories and Shorts. The free sample is 15 seconds; on paid plans you choose 15, 20 or 30 seconds.
 Does Kineo post or run my ads? No. You download the MP4 and publish or boost it from your own account. Kineo does not buy media or promise sales.
