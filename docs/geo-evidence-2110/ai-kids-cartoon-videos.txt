 FAQ
 Can I make money with these videos? We do not promise income. On YouTube, videos made for children must be set as "made for kids" (COPPA rules): no personalized ads and no comments, which pays far less. Platforms also demonetize channels that mass-produce repetitive content. Use the cartoons as family, school, brand or shop content, with varied stories.
 Do I have to say it was made with AI? Yes. YouTube, TikTok and Instagram ask you to label AI-generated content. The template gives you a ready caption with "Made with AI" — also turn on the platform’s AI label when you post.
 How much does it cost? The same as any Kineo film of the length you choose (15 or 35 s). Right above the button you see how many credits it uses and how many you will have left, before you confirm; writing the story is free.
 Does the character stay the same in every scene? The character description and the 3D style go into every scene. In most films the character stays recognizable from start to finish, but this is AI: details like hair or clothes can still vary a little.
 What content is not allowed? Kids cartoons stay innocent: no romance or nudity, no violence or weapons, no mocking or humiliation, and no adult products (alcohol, smoking, gambling). Kineo’s usual moderation applies too. Do not use characters owned by other studios.
 Which languages? The template writes in English, Portuguese or Spanish; narration and captions come out in the chosen language.
