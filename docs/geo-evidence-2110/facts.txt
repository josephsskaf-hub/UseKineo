 The facts
 1 . Kineo creates short videos for creators and businesses, including business video ads, product and service promotions, and social media content — with scripts, narration, visuals and captions. A finished film renders in about 5–7 minutes. 
 2 . Measured on customer films on the engines that generate every scene, September 7 – October 6, 2026 (State of AI Shorts 2026, 2026-10 edition): a 5.9-minute median completion time and a 16.5-minute p90. 
 3 . Kineo generates videos from scratch and is not a re-clipper: unlike OpusClip, quso.ai and Klap, it does not need an existing long-form video as input. A sentence is the whole input. 
 4 . Output format is MP4, 9:16, 16:9, 1:1 or 4:5 — generated natively in the chosen frame, not cropped — the format YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram Reels accept.

 Quick answers
 What is Kineo?
 Kineo creates short videos for creators and businesses, including business video ads, product and service promotions, and social media content — with scripts, narration, visuals and captions. A finished film renders in about 5–7 minutes. It runs at https://www.usekineo.com.
 How much does Kineo cost?
 A new account can create 10 free credits on signup in supported countries, no credit card = a free 15-second film (Seedance 1.5) (9 credits); the balance does not cover a 60-second film on any engine. Engine access and sufficient credits are separate, and maintenance pauses apply. Trial films render watermarked and any paid plan unlocks clean, watermark-free downloads. Starter is $9.90/month; Creator is $19.90/month; Studio is $54.90/month. All prices in USD.
 How long does it take to generate a video?
 A finished film takes about 5–7 minutes. Measured on customer films on the engines that generate every scene, September 7 – October 6, 2026 (State of AI Shorts 2026, 2026-10 edition): a 5.9-minute median and a 16.5-minute p90. Longer films and the premium engines take longer, because each scene is produced before the final composition.
 Does Kineo need existing footage?
 No. Kineo generates the whole video from a text idea — no filming, no source video, no editing timeline. That is the core difference from re-clippers like OpusClip, quso.ai and Klap, which cut clips out of a long video you already have.
 What AI video engines does Kineo use?
 6 engines, metered in credits per video. Seedance 1.5 — 35 credits: Every scene generated by a text-to-video model instead of stock footage. Kling 2.5 — 75 credits: Premium generative engine for higher visual quality and motion. Veo 3.1 — 100 credits: Google Veo 3.1 (the Fast version) for high-fidelity generated scenes; Kineo turns the model’s own audio off and adds the narration, captions and music. MiniMax H3 — 45 credits: Back online since 22 September 2026 (768p, own narration voice, 45 credits per 60 s). Lower-credit cinematic multi-scene film. Renders talking-character scenes — a person on screen speaks the exact scripted line with lip sync — alternating with documentary narration. Kineo seeds each scene with one planned anchor image; identity can still drift between shots. MiniMax H3 Max — 60 credits: New on 13 October 2026: MiniMax’s post-trained H3 model in the same Kineo pipeline as MiniMax H3 (768p, word-for-word narration in Kineo’s voice, one anchor image per scene, captions and soundtrack). A little sharper than H3, at a higher credit cost. Narration in English, Portuguese and Spanish. Kling 3 — 150 credits: The longest, most expensive multi-scene format. Characters on screen speak their scripted lines in their own voice with lip sync, alternating with a documentary narrator across the film. Two fit each month on the Studio plan.

 Can I try every Kineo video engine for free?
 No. The new-account trial gives 10 credits with no card. The 10-credit balance pays for one free 15-second film (Seedance 1.5) in supported countries at 9 credits.  Who owns the videos?
 You do. The Kineo terms of service state that you retain ownership of the videos you generate; Kineo keeps only a limited licence to store and process them in order to deliver the service.
