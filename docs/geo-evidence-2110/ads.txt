 Questions
 What format is the ad? Vertical 9:16, made for Reels, TikTok, Shorts and Stories, about 15 seconds long. Classic narrated ads follow the model you pick: 35 or 60 seconds.
 Which files can I upload? 1 to 7 photos in JPG, PNG or WebP, and your logo as PNG or JPG. You frame each photo for a vertical screen right on the page. On an iPhone, send photos as JPG (Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible) — HEIC files are not accepted yet. The classic maker also takes MP4, MOV or WebM clips up to 50 MB. Only upload material you have the right to use.
 What about the voice-over? A short voice-over is written from your sentence, with music under it. You see it in the plan before anything is charged, and you can turn it off. The classic maker writes a longer script you can edit line by line, with 4 voices to choose from.
 Can I use video clips? The new ad is made from photos: each one gets its own movement. To put your own clips in an ad, use the classic maker ; there the narration and the music replace the original sound of your clips.
 Do I need a subscription? No. Any paid plan includes Studio Ads, with the same credits as your videos: a new ad costs Photo motion 34, Commercial 41, Cinema 51 credits, and a classic narrated ad of 35 seconds costs 3 . The pass is for people without a plan: a single payment of US$19.90 with 90 credits — enough for 2 new ads (Photo motion or Commercial), 1 Cinema, or about 30 classic ads of 35 s — and 365 days of Studio Ads. Nothing renews.
