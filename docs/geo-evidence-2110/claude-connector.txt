 The tools
 Get Kineo plans, pricing and engines Read only 
 kineo_facts
 Returns Kineo's current product facts as published on usekineo.com: subscription plans with USD prices and monthly credits, the free trial for new accounts, video engines with the credits each one costs, business video ad options, and what Kineo is not suited for. Read-only. Use it when the user asks about Kineo's pricing, plans, credits, trial, engines or capabilities. The optional topic narrows the result (overview, plans, engines, business or all).
 Send a script to Kineo Studio Saves a script · not destructive 
 create_video_handoff
 Saves an approved narration script on Kineo and returns a link (https://www.usekineo.com/go/...) that opens Kineo Studio with the script, duration, frame and engine already filled in. Use it when the user asks to send a finished script to Kineo. This tool does not create an account, render a video or charge anything: the link stays valid for 7 days, and nothing is made until the person opens it and chooses to create the video in their own Kineo account. Script: plain text, up to 5000 characters; section labels on their own line (HOOK:, MICRO REWARD:, ESCALATION:, PAYOFF:) are optional and are not narrated. Narration pace is about 3.1 spoken words per second on standard engines (fast = Kineo 1, seedance = Seedance 1.5, kling = Kling 2.5, veo = Veo 3.1), about 186 words for 60 seconds, and about 2.3 on premium engines (hollywood = Kling 3, h3 = MiniMax H3), about 138 words for 60 seconds. The response includes words (the server's count of spoken words, labels excluded), outcome.kind (at_target or shorter_film) with outcomeMessage, and the link. Invalid input returns an error that names what to change, such as the maximum spoken words for the chosen duration.
 Kineo Studio: your Kineo account inside Claude and ChatGPT Available to every Kineo account 
 Kineo MCP (the server above) writes scripts and hands them to Studio — it never renders or charges. Kineo Studio is the second, separate connector for people who already have a Kineo account: you add it by URL, sign in once, and from then on Claude or ChatGPT places the order and Kineo makes the film in your account, spending your account's credits — the same engines, prices and rules as pressing Generate in Studio. Credits are spent only when a render actually starts; with no plan or balance, the assistant gets a link to the plans instead.
 Kineo Studio connector URL

 Name it “Kineo Studio”, paste the URL below and continue. Claude detects the sign-in on its own.

 Claude opens the Kineo login you already use. Allow the connection and you are back in Claude.

 In ChatGPT Live 
 1 Turn on Developer mode
 In ChatGPT: Settings → Apps & Connectors (called Plugins in some versions) → Advanced settings → turn on Developer mode. Needs a paid ChatGPT plan (Plus or higher).
 2 Add a custom MCP server
 Click Create (or “Add custom MCP server”). Name it “Kineo Studio”, paste the URL below, choose Authentication: OAuth and tick “I trust this application”.
 3 Sign in and allow
 ChatGPT opens the Kineo login you already use. Click Allow on the Kineo screen and you are back in ChatGPT.
 4 Turn it on in a chat
 In a new chat, enable Kineo Studio from the “+” / Developer mode menu, then ask for a film the same way.
 Open Claude's “Add custom connector” dialog ↗ · Tools: kineo_account, kineo_create_video, kineo_video_status, kineo_list_videos. Each render is one film; a 15-second Seedance 1.5 film is the cheapest way to try. To revoke access at any time, remove the connector in the app you connected (Claude or ChatGPT).  What happens after the link
 The link shows the saved script. People without a Kineo account sign up first and come straight back to it; then Studio opens pre-filled and waits. Creating the video is a separate step the person takes in their own Kineo account, under that account's plan and credits. The link works for 7 days, and scripts can be up to 5,000 characters. Current prices are on the pricing page and in the kineo_facts tool.
 Data handling
 kineo_facts stores nothing. create_video_handoff stores only what it receives — the script, length, frame, engine, language and optional title — plus a one-way hash of the calling IP address and the user agent, used for rate limiting. It does not receive or store your Claude conversation, memory or files. Details in the privacy policy .
