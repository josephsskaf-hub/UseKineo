How it works

When you receive it

For the agreed production slot, previews are due within 3 business days after payment, complete materials and script approval. The final batch is due within 2 business days after your consolidated feedback.

We work on one batch at a time. The files arrive together for use across the month. Waiting for materials or approval pauses the schedule.
