What the kit does

Offer on screen

An "Offer" field of up to 24 characters: it shows as a badge on the hook and big on the final card, between the name and the button. The voice says it once. Leave it empty for an ad with no offer.

3 or 5 variations

Same product, photos and offer; each variation changes the script angle and the photo order. That is what Black Friday guides ask for: testing hooks, not betting on one ad.

Ready-made angles

Spoken offer, problem → solution, gift idea, why people choose it, and discovery. No angle invents a price, deadline, rating or testimonial.

Frequently asked questions

Can I add the deadline of the sale?

Yes, if it fits in the 24-character Offer field (for example "Until Nov 27"). The script accepts exactly that number or date and no other.

Do the variations repeat the same text?

No. Each one gets a different angle and a different photo order, from the same product and the same offer.

When should I make my Black Friday ads?

Guides recommend finished creatives by October 31 and tests from November 1 to 7, so the winners are known before Black Friday week.
