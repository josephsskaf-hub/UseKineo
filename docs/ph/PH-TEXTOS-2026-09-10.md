## 5 RESPOSTAS PRONTAS

Cole a resposta, não a pergunta. Adapte a primeira frase ao que a pessoa
escreveu — resposta colada crua é visível.

### 1 · "Why is there no free trial / why do I have to pay $1?"
```
Fair question. We ran a free tier for months: 1,849 accounts came in and 12
of them ever paid. The free tier was not bringing customers, it was bringing
render bills — every free film costs us real money at the provider.

So the door is $1 for 7 days now. You get the full Creator plan, 80 credits
up front, and no watermark on what you export. Cancel inside the week and $1
is all you spent. It filters for people who actually want to make something,
and it lets me keep the good engines switched on for them.

After the week: Starter $14/mo, Creator $29/mo, Studio $59/mo. The trial
lands you on Creator, so nothing changes unless you move.
```

### 2 · "Do the videos have a watermark?"
```
No, not on anything you pay for — including the $1 trial. The trial is a real
paid plan (Stripe subscription in trial), so the renders come out clean from
the first one. You download the MP4 and post it wherever you want; the FAQ on
the landing page says the same thing in one line: you own them.

The only watermarked films left are the ones on my own promo account, which I
mark on purpose.
```

### 3 · "Which models are behind it? Is this just a wrapper?"
```
Eight engines, and the badge on every video says which one rendered it — I
would rather lose the sale than fake that.

Credits for one 60-second film:
Kineo 1 (ours) 5 · Seedance 1.5 25 · MiniMax H3 45 · Kling 2.5 50
Veo 3.1 100 · Avatar 110 · Kling 3 150 · Omni Flash 150

The engine is the camera. What Kineo does is the directing: it splits your
paragraph into scenes, writes a shot for each one, keeps the subject
consistent across them, narrates in a real voice, scores it, burns the
captions and cuts to length. That part is the product, and it is why the
same engine gives you a film here and a 5-second clip elsewhere.
```

### 4 · "Can I use these commercially? On a monetised channel?"
```
Yes. You own what you generate — download the MP4 and post it anywhere,
including monetised channels. That is written on the landing page FAQ, not
buried in the terms.

One practical note if you are chasing TikTok's Creator Rewards: it only pays
on videos over a minute, so pick the 60s length in the Studio. Most of the
films in the gallery are 60–66s for exactly that reason.
```

### 5 · "How is this different from OpusClip / HeyGen?"
```
Different job entirely, and I say that as someone who uses both.

OpusClip needs a long video you already recorded — it finds the good moments
and reframes them. There is nothing for it to clip if all you have is an idea.
HeyGen puts a synthetic presenter on screen reading your script; the avatar is
the shot.

Kineo starts from the paragraph and there is no footage and no presenter — it
generates the shots. Closest honest framing: OpusClip cuts what exists,
HeyGen films a person who does not exist, Kineo directs a film that does not
exist yet.

(Their pricing and free-tier details, as verified on their own pages on
July 26 2026, are on our comparison pages if anyone wants the table.)
```

## O QUE NÃO PODE APARECER EM LUGAR NENHUM

- **"nine engines"** — são oito enquanto `S25_PUBLIC=false`.
- **"free"**, "free tier", "start free" — não existe desde 08/09.
- **Preço antigo** ($7 / $15 / $19 / $29 de Studio). Hoje é
  Starter $14 · Creator $29 · Studio $59.
- **`producthunt.com/products/kineo`** — é do concorrente homônimo. O nosso
  slug é `kineo-ai`, e ele só existe depois que o fundador criar a ficha.
- **"3 minutes" para Kling 3 ou Veo 3.1** — nesses, é 10–15 min em hora cheia.
  A promessa de 3 minutos vale para Kineo 1 e Seedance.
- **Contagem de filmes por plano que o plano não libera** — Starter e Creator
  não abrem Kling/Veo/Kling 3/H3/Omni/Avatar em conta nova
  (`lib/enginePlanGate.ts`).
