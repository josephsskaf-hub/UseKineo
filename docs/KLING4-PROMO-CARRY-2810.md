# Kling 4.0 — accepted jobs continued on later UTC days

**DECISÃO APROVADA — implementação do teto do fundador, 29/10/2026:** an already accepted free film/ad may need a new fal call on a later UTC day. That later day's shared US$100 promotion budget must admit the continuation before the provider POST. The old reservation remains consumed and the continuation does not consume another one of the person's three daily entitlement uses.

**FATO CONFIRMADO / IMPLEMENTADO:** `lib/kling4PromoCarry.ts:12` exposes `ensureKling4PromoBudgetToday(admin,{userId,jobId})`. The caller supplies no amount, plan, subscription, free marker or date. Only a positive service-only RPC response with a bounded integer reservation permits a new call. Errors, absent migration, denied cap and malformed data return false.

**FATO CONFIRMADO / IMPLEMENTADO:** `supabase/migrations/20261029033826_kling4_promo_carry_daily_budget.sql:23` adds the security-invoker RPC `ensure_kling4_promo_budget_today`, with empty search path. It requires the original service ledger row to be free, `reason='ok'` and its positive reserved amount equal to the estimated amount. Same original UTC day needs no extra budget. A later day conservatively re-holds the **entire original upper bound**, rather than guessing which portion remains. A carry identity `(user,job,day)` is immutable and idempotent; a transaction lock plus `FOR UPDATE` on the same `kling4_promo_days` row serializes it against both other carries and fresh admissions.

**FATO CONFIRMADO / IMPLEMENTADO:** original days and `kling4_promo_user_days` are never reduced or incremented by continuation. `kling4_promo_carry_days` has RLS and service-only select/insert grants; no update/delete grant or account-delete cascade can reset its history. Its event `kling4_promo_carry_reserved` is blocked in `/api/events`, marked `continuation=true`, `new_entitlement_use=false`, `cost_basis=reserved_upper_bound` and commits atomically with today's reserve.

**SUGESTÃO / integration rule:** call the helper immediately before **each new fal POST**, including a fallback slot, voice, image or music submission. A phase containing several POSTs can cross UTC midnight, so admission once before a long loop is insufficient. Do not call it for read-only polls/downloads. On false preserve the accepted job, submit nothing new and report pending capacity honestly. Already agreed free work must not silently charge credits. A no-promotion paid job does not use this free-promotion carry helper.

**FATO CONFIRMADO / accounting meaning:** a carry reserves the full original bound again even if only a small fallback remains. This is deliberately conservative and can postpone work before actual spend reaches US$100. Carry and original reservations are **upper bounds**, not fal invoices or newly delivered films. Do not count carry events as new subscriptions, new people or new entitlement uses, nor present their sum as invoiced cost.
