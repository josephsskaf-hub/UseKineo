## Fichas de lançamento

**SUGESTÃO.** Cada ficha começa com uma foto autorizada de um adulto, em luz boa, com rosto e cabelo visíveis. A equipe corta um único plano vertical do acervo aprovado, confere a origem do arquivo e ausência de marcas, e mantém a fonte fora da vitrine até validar a nova versão. Se um plano contínuo não tiver os segundos da ficha, a ficha volta à edição. Os valores estimam só o Kling 4.0 Edit em 720p para **uma** conferência por ficha, de acordo com `custoFalKling4` em `lib/recreate/seuVideo.ts:120–127` e a montagem de um trecho em `lib/recreate/invasor.ts:32–33`. Tarifas vigentes na conta, foto/juiz, transcodificação, armazenamento e eventuais repetições ficam fora desses valores. Gasto executado nesta entrega, zero.

### Prompts de prova

1. **One-turn outfit** — `Replace the main person in @Video1 with the person from @Image1. Match their face and hair. Keep the exact outfit before and after the turn, the gesture, timing, camera, framing, background and light. No new branding or text.`
2. **Runway turn** — `Replace the runway walker in @Video1 with the person from @Image1. Match the face and hair. Keep the original clothes, full-body framing, walk, turn, footfalls and lights. Do not add text or logos.`
3. **Hold the dunk** — `Replace only the player who makes the basket in @Video1 with the person from @Image1. Match the face and hair. Keep the ball flight, hoop, jump, landing, clothing, camera and lights. Do not change other people or add logos.`
4. **Confetti selfie loop** — `Replace only the person holding the phone in @Video1 with the person from @Image1. Match their face and hair. Keep the hand motion, phone, background people, confetti timing, clothing, shot and lights unchanged.`
5. **Celebrate with the dog** — `Replace only the main human who celebrates in @Video1 with the person from @Image1. Keep the dog exactly as shot, including its breed, markings and movement. Preserve the ball, celebration, clothes, timing, framing and light.`
6. **Reveal the trick** — `Replace the presenter in @Video1 with the person from @Image1. Keep the prop and its reveal timing, hand actions, original clothes, camera, framing, light and background exactly as shot. Preserve every other person.`
