How Much Do YouTube Shorts Pay? (2026)

The honest answer: YouTube Shorts pay an estimated $0.03 to $0.10 per 1,000 views — roughly $30 to $100 per 1,000,000 views.

No figure on this page is a promise of earnings. Shorts RPM varies widely and YouTube does not publish per-view rates.

How the Shorts payout model actually works

Ads run in the Shorts feed between clips, and the revenue goes into a shared pool. Creators keep 45% of their allocated revenue.

The 2026 requirements to get paid at all

After · present separated from future

Current Shorts monetization requirements

Through January 31, 2027: 1,000 subscribers + 10M qualified public Shorts views, or 4,000 qualified long-form hours.

Current official YPP requirements

Effective February 1, 2027

New entry: 1,000 subscribers + 20M qualified Shorts views or 8,000 long-form hours. Existing YPP status is distinguished.
